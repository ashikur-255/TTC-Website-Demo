Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about TTC and our training programs.

TTC stands for Technical Training Center. It provides technical and vocational training programs designed to develop practical skills for employment and professional development.

TTC can provide training in areas such as web development, computer applications, information technology, database management and other technical skills.

Students, job seekers, professionals and anyone interested in developing technical skills can contact TTC to learn about available training opportunities.

You can contact TTC by phone, email or by submitting the contact form available on the Contact Us page.

Training programs can include practical exercises and hands-on activities to help learners apply their knowledge and develop useful technical skills.

TTC is presented in this project as a training center located in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Replace this information with your actual TTC address when submitting the project.
